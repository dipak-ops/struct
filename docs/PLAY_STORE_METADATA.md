## Full Description

```text
Struct is an offline-first decentralized messaging application that lets nearby smartphones communicate directly over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) without cellular service, Wi-Fi infrastructure, or centralized servers.

Whether you are off-grid on a trail, experiencing network congestion at a festival, or responding to an outage, Struct forms an ad-hoc local mesh network:

• DIRECT & MULTI-HOP RELAY
Communicate directly with nearby devices or relay encrypted packets across intermediate Struct nodes to extend coverage.

• AUTHENTICATED END-TO-END ENCRYPTION
Messages are encrypted end-to-end using ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption with ephemeral X25519 key agreements. Intermediate relay nodes forward encrypted bytes and cannot read message contents.

• LOCAL-FIRST IDENTITY & STORAGE
Your cryptographic Ed25519 identity key is generated locally and stored securely in your device's hardware enclave. Message history is saved only to your local device database.

• STORE-AND-FORWARD DELIVERY
If a recipient is temporarily out of range, intermediate nodes can securely hold encrypted packets and forward them once connectivity is re-established.

• PEER VERIFICATION
Verify peer identities out-of-band using human-readable safety fingerprints or QR code comparison to prevent impersonation.

• TECHNICAL REALITIES & LIMITATIONS
Struct is designed for localized communication. Radio range depends on device hardware, battery levels, physical walls, and RF interference (typical indoor range: 10–25 meters). Struct relies on nearby active devices and does NOT replace government emergency services (911/112).
```
