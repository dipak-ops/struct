## Promotional Text & Description

**Promotional Text**:
`Communicate locally with nearby devices over Bluetooth Low Energy. No cellular internet or accounts required.`

**Description**:
```text
Struct is an offline-first decentralized mesh messenger that lets nearby devices communicate directly over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) without relying on cellular data, Wi-Fi hotspots, or central accounts.

KEY CAPABILITIES:

• OFFLINE MESH RELAY
Send encrypted messages directly to nearby nodes or route packets across intermediate devices to extend localized coverage.

• AUTHENTICATED END-TO-END ENCRYPTION
Protected with ChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated encryption and ephemeral X25519 key agreements. Intermediate relays forward opaque bytes and cannot read your messages.

• LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY
Private keys are isolated in your device's Secure Enclave/Keychain. No accounts, phone numbers, or central servers.

• STORE-AND-FORWARD
Messages waiting for an offline destination are held in bounded relay queues and delivered when the peer reconnects.

• SAFETY NUMBER VERIFICATION
Compare Base32 Crockford fingerprints or scan peer QR codes to verify identity keys out-of-band.

REAL-WORLD CONSTRAINTS:
Communication range is governed by 2.4 GHz radio propagation and physical obstacles (walls, terrain). iOS background peripheral advertising is subject to Apple operating system power management policies. Struct does not replace official emergency services.
```
